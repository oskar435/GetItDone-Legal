Privacy Policy for GetItDone

Effective date: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GetItDone ("the app," "we," "us") handles information when you use it. The short version: GetItDone doesn't have a backend, doesn't require an account, and doesn't collect or transmit your personal data anywhere. Everything you save in the app stays on your device.

1. No account, no backend

GetItDone does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use it. There is no server operated by us that the app talks to for its core functionality. All of your data — categories, folders, saved video clips, notes, thumbnails, and progress-tracking entries — is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. We never see it, never receive a copy of it, and have no way to access it.

If you delete the app, or clear its data, everything you've saved in it is gone — we don't keep a backup anywhere, because we never had a copy to begin with.

2. Information the app accesses on your device

To do its job, GetItDone interacts with a few things on your phone:

  • Photos and videos. When you choose to import a video, GetItDone opens your phone's built-in photo picker so you can select a video you've already downloaded (for example, one saved from TikTok). The app receives only the specific file you pick — it does not have access to the rest of your photo or video library, and it never scans, uploads, or otherwise reads anything you don't select.
  • Receiving shared videos from other apps. GetItDone can appear in your phone's share menu so you can share a downloaded video directly into it from another app. This only works for actual video files — GetItDone is not able to receive plain links or text through this feature.
  • Clipboard (optional convenience). When you're on the video-trim screen, GetItDone checks your clipboard for a TikTok-looking link so it can offer to pre-fill the caption-import field for you, instead of requiring you to paste it manually. This check happens only on that screen, only looks for a TikTok URL pattern, and nothing you copy is sent anywhere as a result of this check alone.

None of the above ever leaves your device. They exist purely so the app can function.

3. The one thing that does leave your device

GetItDone has exactly one feature that talks to the internet: importing a video's caption from TikTok. If you choose to paste a TikTok video link into the caption-import field (or let the app pre-fill it from your clipboard, per section 2), the app sends that link to TikTok's own public "oEmbed" endpoint — a service TikTok provides for exactly this purpose — to retrieve that video's public caption text. TikTok's servers receive the link you provided; GetItDone does not send anything else (no device identifiers, no other app data). This only happens when you actively use the caption-import feature — it is never automatic.

Handling of the data you send to TikTok through this request is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy, not ours.

Beyond this one feature, GetItDone makes no other network requests. It contains no advertising, no analytics or usage-tracking services, and no third-party SDKs that collect data on our behalf.

4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, and have no way to collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (you never create an account)
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics data
  • The contents of your video library beyond the specific clips you choose to import
  • Any usage statistics about how you use the app

5. Children's privacy

GetItDone is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children, consistent with the fact that we do not collect personal information from anyone.

6. Your control over your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, you are always in full control of it:

  • You can delete any clip, folder, or piece of data at any time from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data along with it.
  • There is no account to delete and no server-side copy to request removal of, because none exists.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy — for example, if a future version of the app adds a new feature that changes how data is handled — we'll update this page and change the effective date above. We'd encourage checking back here if you have concerns, especially before or after updating the app.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: get.it.done.productivity@gmail.com

If you email us, we receive the address you write from and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and, if you ask us to, to let you know when the app becomes available for your phone. We do not share it or use it for marketing.
