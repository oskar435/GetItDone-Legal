Privacy Policy for GetItDone

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GetItDone ("the app," "we," "us") handles information when you use it. The short version: GetItDone doesn't have a backend, doesn't require an account, and doesn't collect your personal data. Everything you save in the app stays on your device. The only thing the app itself ever sends over the internet is a TikTok link, when you share it into the app, paste it, or ask the app to read its caption (see section 3). The only other thing that can leave your phone is an email that you choose to send us yourself (see sections 2 and 8).

1. No account, no backend

GetItDone does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use it. There is no server operated by us that the app talks to for its core functionality. All of your data — folders, saved videos and images, links, notes, thumbnails, progress-tracking entries and your settings — is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. We never see it, never receive a copy of it, and have no way to access it.

If you delete the app, or clear its data, everything you've saved in it is gone — we don't keep a backup anywhere, because we never had a copy to begin with. The only exception is a backup file that you save yourself (see section 2): that file is yours to keep, and we never receive it.

2. Information the app accesses on your device

To do its job, GetItDone interacts with a few things on your phone:

  • Photos and videos. When you choose to import a video or an image, GetItDone opens your phone's built-in photo picker so you can select a file you've already saved (for example, a video downloaded from TikTok). The app receives only the specific file you pick — it does not have access to the rest of your photo or video library, and it never scans, uploads, or otherwise reads anything you don't select.
  • Receiving shared content from other apps. GetItDone can appear in your phone's share menu (on iPhone it appears as "Save to GetItDone") so you can share a video, an image, a link or some text directly into it from another app. What you share is saved on your device only. On iPhone, the share menu hands what you share to the app through a storage area on your device that only GetItDone and its share menu entry can use. If you share a TikTok link, the app also reads that video's caption — see section 3.
  • Clipboard (optional convenience). On Android, when you're on the video-trim screen, GetItDone checks your clipboard for a TikTok-looking link so it can offer to pre-fill the caption-import field for you, instead of requiring you to paste it manually. This check happens only on that screen and only looks for a TikTok URL pattern. On iPhone this automatic check is switched off. On every phone, the Paste buttons next to link boxes read your clipboard only when you tap them. Nothing you copy is sent anywhere as a result of reading the clipboard alone.
  • Opening links. When you tap "Open link" on an entry, or tap a web address written in a note, your phone opens it in your browser or in the app it belongs to. GetItDone itself sends nothing when it does this; what happens next is governed by that website's or app's own privacy policy. Only web addresses that start with http or https are ever opened.
  • Backups. Settings has a "Backup & space" screen. If you choose to save a backup, GetItDone creates a file on your phone with your folders, entries, notes, links, captions and progress, and opens your phone's share menu so you can choose where to keep it (for example Google Drive, Files or an email to yourself). GetItDone does not send the file anywhere itself; where it goes is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies to it. You can choose to include your videos and images in the backup, or to save only the notes; with videos and images the file can be several gigabytes. If you choose "Restore from a backup", the app reads the file you pick, adds its contents to what is already in the app, and copies any videos and images in it into the app's storage. Nothing is sent or read unless you choose it.
  • Sending feedback. Settings has a "Send feedback" button. It opens your own email app with a prepared message addressed to us, which includes the app version and your phone's system type and version. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app, and you can edit or delete anything in the message first.

Apart from sending feedback or saving a backup file yourself, none of the above ever leaves your device. They exist purely so the app can function.

3. The one thing the app itself sends over the internet

GetItDone has exactly one feature that talks to the internet: reading a TikTok video's caption. This happens:

  • when you share a TikTok link into GetItDone from another app;
  • when you tap "Read the caption from TikTok" next to a TikTok link in Quick add; and
  • when you paste a TikTok link into the caption-import field on the video-trim screen (or, on Android, let the app pre-fill it from your clipboard, as described in section 2).

It never happens for a link you haven't shared, pasted, or asked the app to read.

When it happens, the app requests that video's public web page from TikTok — the same page a browser would show for that link — reads the caption from it, and keeps only the caption text and the video's own web address (so that the same video can be recognised later). TikTok's servers receive the link and the technical details that every web request carries, such as your device's internet (IP) address and the type of browser the app identifies itself as. GetItDone sends nothing else: no device identifiers, no other app data, nothing from your library.

Handling of the data TikTok receives through this request is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy, not ours.

Beyond this one feature, GetItDone makes no other network requests. It contains no advertising, no analytics or usage-tracking services, and no third-party SDKs that collect data on our behalf.

4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, and have no way to collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (you never create an account)
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics data
  • The videos, images, links, notes and captions you save, or the contents of your photo and video library beyond the specific files you choose to import
  • Any usage statistics about how you use the app

5. Children's privacy

GetItDone is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children, consistent with the fact that we do not collect personal information from anyone.

6. Your control over your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, you are always in full control of it:

  • You can delete any clip, folder, or piece of data at any time from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data along with it. If you want to keep your folders, notes, links (and, if you choose, your videos and images), save a backup file first (Settings > Backup & space); you can add it back later with "Restore from a backup".
  • There is no account to delete and no server-side copy to request removal of, because none exists.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy — for example, if a future version of the app adds a new feature that changes how data is handled — we'll update this page and change the effective date above. We'd encourage checking back here if you have concerns, especially before or after updating the app.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: get.it.done.productivity@gmail.com

If you email us, we receive the address you write from and whatever you include in your message (including the app version and phone details of the prepared feedback message, if you use it). We use it only to reply to you and, if you ask us to, to let you know when the app becomes available for your phone. We do not share it or use it for marketing, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
