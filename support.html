GetItDone Support

GetItDone saves your videos, images, links and notes into folders you build yourself. It has no account and no server: everything is stored on your phone.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: get.it.done.productivity@gmail.com. In the app you can also open Settings > Send feedback, which prepares an email with the app version filled in.

Common questions

How do I get a video into the app? Save the video on your phone with the other app's own Save button, then share it into GetItDone from your gallery or Photos app (on iPhone, choose "Save to GetItDone"), or tap "Add video" in the app and pick it. You can also share a link, or use "Quick add" for a title and a link without a video.

Where is my data? Only on your phone. We never receive it and cannot recover it. If you delete the app, your data goes with it.

How do I keep a copy? Open Settings > Backup & space and save a backup file, with or without your videos and images. Keep it somewhere safe, such as Google Drive, Files or an email to yourself. You can add it back later with "Restore from a backup".

The phone is almost full. In Settings > Backup & space you can see how much room your videos use and delete the files that no entry needs.
